Flyhug Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Flyhug (“the game”, “we”) is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad, Apple Vision Pro and Android. This policy explains what information is involved when you play.

What the game itself stores

  • Your progress (levels, stars, seeds, garden decor, Bird Book, Scrapbook, settings and the flock in progress) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers, and we don’t receive or see this data.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as Apple’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or Android’s Advertising ID, only where you allow it
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language)
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped)

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, ads are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Europe, the UK and some US states: you’ll see Google’s consent form the first time you open the game. You can change your choices any time from Settings › Ad privacy choices inside the game.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s Google settings.
  • Rewarded ads (for a hint, extra feathers or double seeds) are always optional.

Children

Flyhug is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
